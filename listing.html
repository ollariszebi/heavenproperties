About this property

Set on the western frond of Palm Jumeirah, this villa opens directly onto a private stretch of beach, with the Dubai Marina skyline drawn across the horizon beyond the water.

The ground floor is arranged around a double-height living space that folds open to the terrace, so the pool, the beach and the interior read as a single room in the cooler months. A separate kitchen sits behind the show kitchen, and a guest suite occupies its own wing with a private entrance.

Upstairs, four bedrooms each have their own terrace and bathroom. The principal suite takes the full width of the seaward side, with a dressing room, a study, and an outdoor shower facing the water.

The property is handed over fully furnished and is managed by our own team, which makes it equally straightforward as a residence or as a short-let investment.
