How we got here

2012
The background

Built on finance. Proven in property.

Miklós Richárd Nagy and Ábrahám Kovács came to real estate from finance, and have been working in the market since 2012. Every project is assessed the way a portfolio position is assessed: location, build quality, payment structure and long-term value. That method still decides what we put in front of a client.

2024
The brand

HEAVEN. brings it under one roof.

What had been separate strands of work — brokerage, rental management, legal and residency — became one organisation under the HEAVEN. name, so a client deals with one team instead of four.

2026
On the international stage

Representing the UAE abroad.

At LPS Shanghai, one of the world’s most prestigious luxury property shows, we present UAE investment opportunities to investors from across Asia. As invited experts at Portfolio, Hungary’s leading business and finance platform, we also take part in the conferences that shape the market.

2027
Next

American capital, UAE opportunities.

An investor event in Miami lays the groundwork for the USA joining the ecosystem: the same brokerage, rental management and administrative back office, for investors who buy across borders and expect one point of contact.

About us, answered.

Four divisions, one ecosystem, and a team in the market since 2012. These are the questions clients ask before they commit.

Talk to an advisor →
Who founded HEAVEN. Properties?
Miklós Richárd Nagy and Ábrahám Kovács, both from finance rather than estate agency. They have been working in property since 2012; the HEAVEN. brand brought their divisions under one roof in 2024. That background is why every project is assessed like a portfolio position: location, build quality, payment structure and long-term value.
Is HEAVEN. Properties a trustworthy agency for UAE property investment?
Every advisor holds RERA, DLD and escrow certification, and every deal passes a quality-assurance review before it reaches you. We work with more than 90% of the UAE’s leading developers, and the track record behind that is 1,500+ handovers and 273,000 m² transacted since 2012.
Can I buy a UAE property from Europe?
Yes. Our European base in Budapest exists for exactly that, and HEAVEN. Consulting handles the administrative side — company formation, bank accounts, Emirates ID, visa processing and legal representation — so you deal with one organisation rather than five.
What is the Heaven Standard?
Five commitments we hold to without exception: a personal reply within 24 hours, a tailored proposal within 72 hours, one dedicated advisor throughout, transparent administration with complete documentation, and continued support after handover.
Which companies make up HEAVEN. Global?
Four divisions. HEAVEN. Properties handles brokerage, HEAVEN. Livings runs rental management, HEAVEN. Consulting carries the administrative and legal back office, and HEAVEN. Motion is our in-house lead generation.
Where is HEAVEN. expanding?
Spain and Thailand open in 2026, joining the UAE and Hungary. The USA follows in 2027, with the groundwork already laid through an investor event in Miami.
