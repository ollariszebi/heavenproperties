Frequently asked questions

Buying abroad raises questions long before it raises paperwork. Here are the ones we hear most — and if yours isn't here, just ask.

Talk to an advisor →
Which countries do you operate in?
We currently work across four destinations: Dubai in the United Arab Emirates, Hungary, Spain and Thailand. Each market is covered by a local team, so the advice you get reflects how that market actually works.
Can foreign buyers purchase property in these markets?
In most cases yes, though the rules differ by country and change over time — from designated freehold areas to restrictions on certain property types. We walk you through what applies to your situation before you commit to anything.
Does buying a property come with residency or visa benefits?
Some markets link residency programmes to property investment above a certain value. These programmes are set by governments and their conditions are revised periodically, so we confirm the current requirements with you case by case rather than promising a fixed outcome.
Can you help arrange financing?
We introduce you to banks and brokers who lend to international buyers in each market, and help prepare what they ask for. The lending decision and terms always sit with the lender.
How long does a purchase usually take?
A completed resale property typically closes in a few weeks once terms are agreed. Off-plan purchases follow the developer's payment schedule and run until handover, which can be a matter of years.
Do you manage the property after the purchase?
Yes — furnishing, letting, maintenance and resale when the time comes. Many of our clients buy in a country they don't live in, so ongoing management is part of what we do rather than an afterthought.
